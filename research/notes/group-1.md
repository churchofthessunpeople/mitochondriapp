### Key terms he uses
- **Planetary Solvency / Insolvency** — whether a planet's internal magnetic "capital" can buffer cosmic inputs; insolvency forces the planet to liquidate climate stability and biology.
- **Reciprocal Magnetic Vortices / "Master Gears"** — his name for the Great Red Spot and the SAA as paired dynamo features.
- **SCRAM (Safety Control Rod Axe Man)** — nuclear emergency-shutdown term he uses for automated planetary (atmospheric) and cellular (biological) shutdowns.
- **Atmospheric SCRAM** — climate extremes (AMOC stall, polar-vortex drift, Rossby-wave overclocking) read as the atmosphere moving heat after magnetic failure.
- **Biological SCRAM** — mitochondria deforming and leaking mtDNA, which triggers cGAS-STING and the Cell Danger Response.
- **Galactic Line-Filter** — the local interstellar hydrogen bubble (21 cm / 1420 MHz line) he says once shielded the solar system and paced a 21 Hz ionospheric harmonic.
- **21 Hz harmonic** — an ionospheric resonance he links to neural grounding and telomere pacing.
- **Zeeman Handshake** — the planetary magnetic field's alignment of electron spins in mitochondrial cytochromes, tied to agonic lines.
- **Agonic line** — a line of zero magnetic declination; he treats it as a grounding/pinning reference.
- **CISS (Chiral Induced Spin Selectivity)** — spin filtering of electrons by chiral molecules; he says it lets electrons flow down the IMM without "magnetic friction".
- **IMM 30 MV/m force field** — the inner mitochondrial membrane's electric field strength, which he says breaches when pinning is lost.
- **Dielectric constant 160 vs. 78** — structured (exclusion-zone) water near membranes (~160) vs. ordinary bulk water (78); the drop marks failure.
- **EZ water** — exclusion-zone, liquid-crystalline structured water lining membranes.
- **Marcus Inversion bottleneck** — from Marcus electron-transfer theory; he uses it for electron-transport logjams where higher driving force slows transfer.
- **UPE (ultraweak photon emission)** — faint light from cells; coherent and spin-polarized in health, chaotic noise in disease.
- **DAMPs** — damage-associated molecular patterns (leaked mtDNA, formylated proteins) that trigger inflammation.
- **cGAS-STING** — the cytosolic DNA-sensing immune pathway he says is activated by leaked mtDNA.
- **Cell Danger Response (CDR)** — Robert Naviaux's concept of a cellular defensive metabolic state.
- **Biological Nazaré** — his metaphor (after Nazaré's giant waves) for the cristae's proton-focusing wave engine that spins ATP synthase.
- **Nitride cage** — nitrogen-rich ring structures (porphyrins, NAD) he sees as light-capturing semiconductors.
- **Nicotinamide shuttle** — his model of NAD+/NADH as a light-storing and light-emitting relay to Complex I (260/340/460 nm).
- **Natural genetic engineering** — his principle that environment and mitochondrial physics, not DNA sequence, decide gene expression.
- **Spinning tire (PSI/RPM) heuristic** — magnetic flux as internal "pressure" that sustains planetary rotation.
- **Rossby wave overclocking** — his term for an atmosphere speeding up planetary-scale waves to move heat.
- **Wind Shear Guillotine** — high-altitude shear that tears apart developing hurricanes.
- **Universal Scaling Law** — the gas giant → Earth → Venus → Mars continuum mapped onto healthy → stressed → cancerous → necrotic cells.
- **Bh (horizontal magnetic pinning vector)** — the field component flat to the crust, which he considers protective; a vertical (Bz) tilt is harmful.
- **IMJ "bus bar"** — the inter-mitochondrial junction, which he treats as a power-distribution rail.
- **Hutton 14-Å limit** — the maximum distance he cites for efficient electron tunneling between complexes.
- **Berry phase** — a geometric quantum phase acquired on closed paths; he proposes it as mitochondrial memory.
- **71 THz / 613 THz** — frequencies he assigns to the circadian timing signal and to an aromatic UV "core" tied to consciousness.
- **MITF-AMPAR pathway** — a pathway he says is activated by sunlight-driven mitochondrial stabilization, driving "radiosynthetic" growth.
- **Deuterium congestion** — heavy-hydrogen buildup that he says jams ATP synthase and pushes cells toward Warburg metabolism.
- **Warburg shift** — cancer cells' reliance on glycolysis; framed as decay in one post and as adaptive memory in another.
- **Brachistochrone / least-time path** — the fastest-descent curve; he says cristae shape electron paths along it.
