### Key terms he uses
- **Agonic line** — where magnetic north and true north coincide (0° declination); he treats living on it as protective.
- **Magnetic declination** — the angle between true and magnetic north; he links large east or west values to autism.
- **Spatial aliasing** — his term for the brain's spatial map getting "smeared" by geometric magnetic distortion.
- **Thalamic tri-axis (sphenoid X, vagus Y, heart Z)** — his model of the thalamus as a 3D positioning system aligned to body axes.
- **cGAS-STING** — an innate immune pathway triggered by DNA in the cytoplasm; he invokes it after mitochondrial DNA leaks.
- **VDR brake** — his description of the vitamin D receptor's role in the mitochondrial membrane, preventing Marcus inversion.
- **Coupled vs uncoupled haplogroups** — Wallace's idea that some maternal lineages favour ATP output, others heat production.
- **High-Q plutonic anchor** — deep, slowly cooled magnetic rock that he says holds the local field steady.
- **Bh / Bz** — horizontal and vertical components of the local magnetic field.
- **DRT3 / DRT7** — template-free DNA-writing enzymes he says let cells adapt quickly to radiation.
- **Interstitial telomeric loop (ITL)** — telomere-like repeats inside chromosome 2 at the fusion site, which he calls a fuse or resonator.
- **Telomere pacing by mitochondria** — his claim that coherent mitochondrial signals keep nuclear telomeres stable.
