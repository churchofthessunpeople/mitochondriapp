## Core model

Kruse treats the mitochondrion as a light- and magnetism-driven electronic device. In his model, healthy cells need three inputs together — full-spectrum sunlight (especially dawn UV-A and near-infrared), contact with the ground, and a stable, mostly horizontal geomagnetic field. With them, water at the inner mitochondrial membrane holds a structured, high-capacitance state he numbers as dielectric constant **160**, the membrane holds about **30 MV/m** (≈ –50 mV), electron spins stay aligned (CISS), electrons tunnel cleanly to oxygen, and Complex IV makes deuterium-depleted water. Remove them — through indoor blue light, wireless radiation (nnEMF), high latitude, high declination, high-rise living, or the planet's weakening field (which, he says, now lets a flood of muons reach the ground under the South Atlantic Anomaly) — and the water collapses to ordinary bulk water (**78**), electron transfer stalls in a **"Marcus inversion,"** singlet oxygen and heat build up, deuterium accumulates, mitochondrial DNA leaks and sets off inflammation (cGAS-STING), and cells fall back to fermentation (the **Warburg shift**). He increasingly frames these retreats as protective: Marcus inversion as a circuit breaker, body fat as a capacitor that buffers voltage and stores deuterium, even cancer as a way to buy time. The same physics, in his account, runs the brain: adenosine signals that the membrane grid is tiring and triggers sleep, myelin recharges as a proton battery overnight, and the felt speed of time tracks how fast electrons tunnel in the thalamus. In his latest posts he ranks magnetism ahead of light and water, treats a person's maternal haplogroup as the blueprint that decides which "magnetic zipcode" suits them, and describes the body's barriers (blood–brain, gut, retina) as isotope filters that fail together. He calls the end state of the water collapse "lattice lock" — stiffened, deuterium-loaded water that he says shows up as a rigid heart rhythm, spinal scarring and sun allergy — and treats carbon dioxide, the vagus nerve, and grounding at sunrise as ways to release it, while reading the planet's own changes (field excursions, volcanic carbon dioxide, even crustal displacement) as the large-scale version of the same story. He traces cancer, autoimmunity, neurodegeneration, autism, infertility, metabolic disease, and heart disease to this one pathway, and holds that drugs, supplements, and diet cannot fix what he calls an environmental "hardware" problem — location, light, and grounding come first.
