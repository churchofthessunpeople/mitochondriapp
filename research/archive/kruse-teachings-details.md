### 1. Mitochondria as electronic and quantum devices
- **1.1 Mitochondria are field-tuned antennas / "quantum synchrotrons."** The inner membrane and its complexes line up with Earth's field; he calls the mitochondrion the Sun's wireless receiver.
- **1.2 Shape is a real-time response, not a disease.** Swelling, fragmentation, donut shapes, megamitochondria, and nanotunnels are the organelle reshaping itself to survive specific electrical, magnetic, or isotopic stress. - **1.3 Electrons must be spin-aligned (CISS) to land on oxygen.** Chiral proteins filter spin; the field "handshake" (his "Zeeman handshake") keeps the filter working.

He adds that the blood–brain, gut, retinal and spinal barriers fail together through this network (see 21.3), and that pole migration causes a "multi-barrier blowout."
- **1.7 Ephaptic coupling is the body's main wireless bus, not leakage.** Terahertz emissions from cytoskeletal "phased arrays" cross 20–50 nm clefts where structured water removes ionic screening; the resulting interference patterns phase-lock metabolism, nerve firing, and morphogenesis — more fundamental, he says, than neurotransmitters.
- **1.8 Cristae are plasmonic antennas.** Iron- and copper-containing respiratory proteins behave like metal-organic frameworks that concentrate weak native light; when cristae lose shape (or the field shifts), the waveguide turns into a leak of incoherent ultra-weak photon emission (UPE) that drives heteroplasmy. Electromagnetism dominates at cell scale, so UPEs matter there. In July he says a devastated magnetic terrain harms everything built by chloroplasts and mitochondria, with non-coherent UPE from diamagnetic oxygen driving high-heteroplasmy disease.
- **1.9 A 71-THz signal paces the nucleus and telomeres.** He reads Song et al. (2026) as a mitochondria-to-nucleus quantum-electrodynamic channel that times telomeres and transcription while the membrane field holds; at dielectric 78 the nucleus "goes blind" and telomeres shorten.

- **3.10 Microgravity is a Marcus inversion.** He reads a 2026 Nature Communications spaceflight study (mitochondrial genes fall with food and air unchanged) through all four forces: cosmic rays flip spins, lost gravity loosens cristae and histones ("epigenetic lockout"), heavy isotopes unravel DNA, and terahertz pacing gives way to primitive UPE; he equates it with Picard's energy-resistance principle. In July he credits Picard with highlighting the Japanese finding and says it raises "energy resistance" even though the chemistry is unchanged (see 22.6).

The Warburg question is a spin (intersystem-crossing) question rather than chemistry; the PI3K/Akt/mTOR axis is a readout of inner-membrane collapse (a "matrix fire"); he names missing NIR, nnEMF, deuterium and "the wrong isotope" of copper as causes, adds that cancer reflects deformed sphenoid geometry and a flattened CSF vortex, and says no targeted drug can fix that.

### 4. Light
- **4.1 Light acts mainly through a non-visual, "optico-magnetic" route.** Citing Hollwich's "energetic portion" of the visual pathway, he says light through eye and skin steers the diencephalic–pituitary system via neuropsin, melanopsin, cholesterol, melanin, water, heme, and collagen; daylight couples the body to cosmic time (Hufeland, Paracelsus, Halberg).

He calls 380 nm the mTOR switch and proton gate, says sunrise UV-A "renovates" heme and melanin, and runs it through a POMC → ACTH/alpha-MSH → VDR–RXR loop that guards the brain.
- **4.3 NAD⁺/NADH is a light-storing semiconductor.** He highlights 260 nm (NAD⁺ absorption), 340 nm (NADH absorption), and 460 nm (NADH emission) as an optical relay to Complex I.

He calls UV-B "term life insurance" for people who aren't magnetically pinned, says not to fear it in cancer, and says lowering the UV index by atmospheric masking would be a permanent winter.

The best red light is the Sun; he criticizes NIR-focused teachers (Fosbury, Shehault, Pollack, Zimmerman, and later Wunsch), saying NIR alone "breaks the rules."
