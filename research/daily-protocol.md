# Daily protocol — Frisco, TX (75035)

Morning to night. Time the day by the sun, not the clock.

**Location**
- Frisco, TX (75035).
- Sun times (mid-October): sunrise ~7:29 AM, solar noon ~1:14 PM, sunset ~7:00 PM CDT.
- Sunrise ranges from ~6:20 AM in June to ~7:30 AM in early winter, so recheck the times monthly.
- Magnetic field: 47.9 µT, 61° dip, 2.5° E declination.
- Ground: backyard, black clay soil, no local basalt.

**Gear**
- Magnetico 20 G sleep pad under the mattress, about 3–4 in below you.
- 36 cm open copper ring.
- TENS 7000, with a left-ear clip electrode and Baja Gold salt water for the clip.
- Salt: Baja Gold sea salt, used everywhere salt appears in this plan.
- Jade stone.
- Earthing.com double-sided mat at the desk, wired from the 2nd-floor window to a ground rod in the yard.
- Second earthing mat on the bed, over the sheet and above the Magnetico, on the same setup.

**Work:** desk work, daytime, standing on the grounded mat.

**Diet:**
- Morning: butter coffee with 2 Kori krill oil softgels.
- One large meal a day, mostly red meat (NY strip, 85/15 ground beef) or wild Alaskan sockeye salmon.
- Water: carbonated reverse-osmosis water from Frisco city water.

## 1. Wake — about 30 minutes before sunrise

**Before getting up**

1. Dream recall to develop any potential insights.
2. Nasal humming, 5–10 minutes, sitting upright:
   - Mouth closed; breathe in through the nose for 4 seconds.
   - Hum low ("MMMM" or "OM") for 6–8 seconds on the exhale.
   - If one side of the nose is blocked, aim the vibration at that side.

**Up**

3. Incandescent or red light only. No overhead LEDs, no phone screen.
4. Light yoga, right after leaving the bedroom: gentle, restorative poses and long static stretches with slow nasal breathing. Do it barefoot on the grounded earthing mat, under the same incandescent or red light.
5. Water for the day, after the morning coffee: carbonated reverse-osmosis water made from Frisco city water.

## 2. Sunrise — first 60–90 minutes of sun

1. Go outside facing east. No sunglasses, glasses or contacts. Expose as much skin as practical.
2. Stand barefoot on raw earth or dewy grass in the backyard for 30–45 minutes, away from the house slab, electrical panel and buried lines. Bare clay in the early morning works well.
3. While standing, breathe slowly through the nose: 4 seconds in, 8 seconds out, humming on the exhale, for 5–10 minutes.
4. After the light, take 60–180 seconds of cold water on bare skin (about 57 °F/14 °C): a shower, a plunge, or a face dunk or ice pack on the face and neck.
   - **Frisco:** tap water is too warm for most of the year. Use a chilled plunge or ice.
5. Optional: ice on the back of the neck or under the chin.

## 3. Morning fuel — after the sunrise block

1. Butter coffee: grass-fed butter, C8 MCT oil and a pinch of Baja Gold sea salt.
2. Take 2 Kori krill oil softgels with the coffee, after the sunrise grounding rather than before it.
3. Optional: 1–2 egg yolks.
4. Use a 40 Hz tuning fork.

## 4. Mid-morning — UV-A window (~9:30 AM to noon)

1. Spend 15–25 minutes outside, walking or moving, eyes and skin uncovered. No sunglasses.
2. Walk slowly among trees and plants when possible.

## 5. Daytime work

1. **Grounding:** stand barefoot on the grounded mat (bare skin, no socks).
   - Keep the ground wire's route away from outlets, power strips and cords.
   - **Frisco:** in dry spells, water the soil around the ground rod; dry, cracked clay conducts poorly.
2. **Wiring:** use wired internet. Move the router, power supplies and chargers away from where you sit or stand. Don't rely on airplane mode.
3. **Light:** open a window for daylight and use incandescent bulbs, not LEDs.
   - At a screen indoors only: optional yellow lenses that filter 400–465 nm. Never wear blue blockers outdoors or in daylight.
4. **Body:**
   - Wear the 36 cm open copper ring around the neck. Keep the gap open and add no magnets.
   - Chew mastic gum, mostly on the right side.
5. **Audio:** use air-tube headphones or a speaker, never Bluetooth.
6. **Headaches:** hold the jade stone (smooth green nephrite) to the temple.
7. **Breaks:** go outside barefoot whenever possible.
8. **Gargling:** gargle hard for 30–60 seconds, once or twice a day.

## 6. Solar noon — when your shadow is shortest

1. **UV-B:** 3–10 minutes of sun on the torso and back, barefoot and grounded.
   - Start at 3 minutes and add a minute every few days.
   - Cover up or move to shade at the first sign of warmth. Never redden.
   - **Frisco:** midday UV is strong from about April to September; build up slowly.
2. **Main meal** near solar noon, outdoors if possible.
   - **Frisco clock times:**
     - Until Nov 1: about 1:00–1:30 PM.
     - Nov 1 to early March (standard time): about 12:15–1:00 PM.
     - Spring and summer: about 1:00–1:45 PM.
   - What to eat:
     - **Red meat:** NY strip steak or 85/15 ground beef. Buy pasture-raised, grass-fed and grass-finished, from Texas ranches where possible.
     - **Fat:** strip and 85/15 are leaner than ribeye. Cook in or top with butter, ghee or beef tallow so fat, not protein, is the main fuel. Swap in ribeye or 80/20 now and then.
     - **Seafood:** wild Alaskan sockeye salmon, regularly. Halibut, sardines and anchovies also work.
     - **Greens:** beet greens, Swiss chard or spinach cooked in tallow, lard, ghee or butter.
     - Local and in season; no out-of-season tropical fruit.
3. Use the 40 Hz tuning fork.

## 7. Afternoon

1. **TENS 7000, right after the main meal,** 10 minutes (first of two daily sessions). Optional: rest on the Magnetico pad during the session.
   - Placement: ear clip on the left tragus or concha, with the return pad behind the same ear; or the solar plexus plus T10–T12 using the stock pads. The ear clip is a separate purchase.
   - TENS 7000 settings:
     - Mode: N (normal, constant).
     - Rate: 25 Hz.
     - Width: 200–250 µs.
     - Timer: manual setting, currently 10 minutes. His range is 15–30 minutes; raise it if you want the full dose.
     - Use one channel only.
   - Intensity: Start at zero, turn up slowly until you just feel it, then back off to a faint tingle or below. Never twitching or pain.
   - Put the black lead nearest the nerve.
   - Contact (ear clip only): wet the clip's felt tips with Baja Gold salt water. If the clip is bare metal or carbon, wet a small piece of felt or cotton pad instead.
     - Mix: about ¼ teaspoon of Baja Gold sea salt in half a cup of water. More salt doesn't help and can irritate the ear.

2. **Exercise:** outdoors and grounded, at moderate intensity, not straight from a screen.
   - Mix intervals with steady effort.
   - Do slow, controlled strength work without holding your breath.
   - **Frisco:** in summer, move it to morning or near sunset.
3. **Buteyko breathing,** 5 minutes: a small breath in and out, a 3–5 second nose-pinch hold, then 10 seconds of easy breathing. Repeat.
## 8. Sunset — last hour of daylight

1. Spend 20–30 minutes outside with bare skin and no sunglasses, watching the light shift from amber to blue.
2. Go barefoot again if possible.

## 9. After dark

1. **Light:** red incandescent bulbs, or glasses that block 100% of blue and green.
2. **Screens:** avoid them. If you can't, wear full blocking glasses and don't eat sugary food in front of them.
3. **Sound:**
   - Put a 128 Hz tuning fork on the sternum or neck.
   - Hum, tone, or play a drum, gong or singing bowl. Use live sound, not speakers.
4. **TENS 7000, 10 minutes, just before bed:** same left-ear placement, settings and salt water as the after-meal session; this is the day's second and last session.
   - Let the 10-minute timer end the session; take the clip off and put the unit away before lights out.
   - Skip it if the main meal was within a couple of hours of bed, or if it leaves you wired or the morning scores drop.
5. **Cold (optional):** a second cold session after dinner.
6. Take the copper ring off.

## 10. Bedtime

1. **Room:**
   - Turn off Wi-Fi and the router; keep the phone out of the room.
   - Make the room totally dark and cold.
   - Sleep grounded on the earthing mat, laid across the foot of the bed with bare feet on it. Never put a cotton layer between skin and mat.
   - No electric adjustable bed.
2. **Bed position:** head to the north, body aligned north–south.
3. **Magnetico pad:** north-seeking side toward you, on for the full night (8 hours), not short sessions.
4. **Before lights out:**
   - Put 3–5 drops of organic, cold-pressed, hexane-free castor oil in the navel.
   - Use mouth tape. An optional Buteyko belt.
   - Play binaural audio through a speaker or air-tube headphones, never Bluetooth: theta (4–7 Hz) for about 20 minutes, then delta (0.5–3.5 Hz) while falling asleep.
5. **HRV tracking:** use a wired or offline device. No Bluetooth trackers or smart rings in the bedroom.
