## Glossary additions: Core model

| Term | Plain meaning (in his usage) |
|---|---|
| Fine-structure constant (about 1/137) | The number that sets how strongly light grips matter; he says structured water tightens that grip and deuterium loosens it |
| Coherent / coherence | Electron spins moving in step, which he says lets enzymes signal instead of burning energy as heat |
| Magnesium-25 | A form of magnesium with a magnetic nucleus that he says lets the field boost ATP production |
| Isotopic flashover | A deuterium nucleus bridging the membrane's huge electric field and shorting it |
| Faraday cage | An electrical shield; his role for melanin near the high-voltage membrane |
| Brachistochrone | The curve along which a falling object arrives fastest; his description of the crista junction's shape |
| Topological insulator | A material that blocks current through its bulk but conducts along its surface; his model of the inner membrane |
| Chiral handshake | His name for the merger of an ancient iron–sulfur motor with a fluid cell body that formed complex cells |
| Chimera / mitochondrial monolith | A person carrying two genetically distinct cell lines / his term for such a person whose cells still share one mitochondrial lineage |
| Magnetic buffer tanks | His description of the clock proteins Period and Cryptochrome as charge stores tuned to Earth's field |
| CD₃ (deuterated methyl) | A methyl tag in which all three hydrogens are deuterium, the heaviest form he says the clock must swap out at night |
| Environmental acidosis | His term for protons flooding cells when structured water collapses, so oxygen is released but cannot be used |
| Bohr effect | Hemoglobin releasing oxygen as tissue becomes more acidic |
| Stator | The fixed part of a motor that sets up its field; his role for Earth's dynamo |
| Glycerol-3-phosphate (G3P) shunt | A side entrance for electrons that bypasses a jammed Complex I, trading voltage for survival ("slow and foggy rather than fast and dead") |
| Complex I | The main entry point of the electron chain, which he says the egg cell keeps switched off |
| SANS | Spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome: swollen optic-nerve sheaths, flattened eyeballs and enlarged brain fluid spaces |
| Relaxation time (T2) | How long protons take to settle after being disturbed; he says reversed local rock stretches it past a "7.2-second quiet buffer" |
| Magnetic null | Local and global fields canceling so the body feels almost no field |
| Acute lattice lock | A sudden lock in an already loaded person when demand spikes, as in exercise or infection |
| Welded exhaust pipe | His mechanic's image for a blocked bicarbonate loop and vagal drain that lets deuterium back up |
| Hyaluronic acid | The slippery gel between tissue layers, which he says deuterium stiffens so layers grind |
| Obex | The lower tip of the fourth ventricle, through which he says deuterium flushes into the spinal canal in ALS |
