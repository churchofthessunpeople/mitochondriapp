## Glossary additions: Light and Water

| Term | Plain meaning (in his usage) |
|---|---|
| FICZ / AhR | A compound UV-A makes from tryptophan in skin / the aryl hydrocarbon receptor it switches on, which he treats as a light antenna |
| Fraunhofer lines | Narrow dark gaps in the sun's spectrum, which he reads as a solar "barcode" |
| Chappuis band | Ozone's broad absorption of yellow-orange light, which he says casts a "shadow" around 589 nm |
| Photonic grammar | His name for the coded pattern ozone and the sun's gaps carve into sunlight, which lets the vitamin D receptor act as a brake |
| Ink without the book | His image for a vitamin D pill: the product without the light-run code that regulates it |
| NADH | The loaded, electron-carrying form of NAD⁺ |
| NMN / NR | NAD⁺-boosting supplements he sees as a chemical bypass of a stalled energy chain |
| Catecholamines | Dopamine, noradrenaline and adrenaline; he says melanin is broken back down into them when it can't be maintained |
| Maillard-orange shift | Melanin or tissue "browning" when overloaded with deuterium |
| Paramagnetic | Drawn into a magnetic field; he says melanin and oxygen both are, so both depend on Earth's field |
| Homocysteine "check engine light" | Rising homocysteine as his sign that light-dependent B vitamins and the interior tan are failing |
| Radiotrophic fungi | Melanised black fungi (as at Chernobyl) that turn ionising radiation into metabolic energy |
| Counterfeit timestamp | His phrase for phone blue light telling the body clock it is always noon |
| Myopia | Short-sightedness; he links its epidemic to eyes losing full-spectrum light |
| Ballast (deuterium) | The small amount of heavy hydrogen he says a vortex needs to hold its spin, which is why he does not aim for zero |
| Kinetic isotope effect | Heavier isotopes reacting more slowly; he says deuterium slows energy-chain and fat-burning enzymes two- to sevenfold |
| G3P shuttle | The glycerol-3-phosphate side route that passes electrons past Complex I, leaving heavy hydrogen behind |
| FADH₂ | An electron carrier that enters the energy chain after Complex I; fat burning routes more electrons through it |
| Internal rigor mortis | Deuterium pulling the far-from-balance ATP synthase motor toward a settled, lifeless state |
| Elovanoids / neuroprotectin D1 | Healing signals made from DHA that he says deuterium mistunes |
| Arachidonic acid | An omega-6 fat that makes inflammatory signals, which he says keep running when DHA signals are jammed |
| AMOC | The Atlantic's great overturning current; he says its slowdown leaves deuterium in East Coast surface water |
| Thixotropic | Thinning as it moves; his description of blood and spinal fluid |
| MCTs (C8) | Medium-chain fats that go straight to the liver and must be burned at once, yielding light water and ketones |
| NHANES | The US national nutrition survey he cites for flat calorie intake alongside rising obesity |
| 100th meridian | John Wesley Powell's humid-East/arid-West line, which he treats as a moving boundary of good water |
