## Glossary additions: new articles and folds

| Term | Plain meaning (in his usage) |
|---|---|
| Bicuspid aortic valve (BAV) | An aortic valve born with two leaflets instead of three; he calls it a "vortex destruction event" that fires blood off-centre. |
| Wall shear stress | The frictional drag of flowing blood on the vessel lining, which he says a centred vortex keeps to a minimum. |
| 4D-flow MRI | A scan that tracks blood motion in three dimensions over time; he cites it showing a bicuspid valve's tight helical jet. |
| Hypomethylation | Too few methyl tags on DNA; he says deuterium jams the tagging enzymes and erases the timing that builds a three-leaflet valve. |
| Satellite mode | His term for a developing egg keeping Complex I switched down to keep deuterium out, like a satellite powering down. |
| 4.3-second stutter | His figure for how often a deuterium atom strikes the system on ordinary 155 ppm water. |
| Speed bump | His word for a single deuterium strike that interrupts the body's coherent signal. |
| Coherence time | The quiet interval between deuterium strikes; he says buying low-deuterium water buys coherence time. |
| Magnetic stall | A sudden hydraulic and electrical standstill he says can mimic brain death. |
| Magnetic de-fragger | His name for rescue tools (epi-pens, dopamine drips, 3% saline boluses) that he says restart a stalled, deuterium-locked system. |
| Levophed | The hospital brand of norepinephrine, a catecholamine he says revived apparently dead ICU patients. |
| Racemic epinephrine | A form of adrenaline; he says high-dose dopamine drips act much like it. |
| MTHFR | A folate-pathway gene whose variants he cites in links to heart outflow defects such as aortic stenosis. |
| Ferron (polarization wave) | A ripple of electric order moving through a ferroelectric material; he says UV-A on melanin launches one through potassium-held cell water (distinct from his use of "ferron" for iron cores). |
| Light ferry boats | His phrase for hormones such as ACTH carrying light and magnetic information, not just chemistry. |
| ACTH | The pituitary hormone cut from POMC that drives the adrenal glands; he says sunrise light helps make it. |
| Transsulfuration | The sulfur pathway that turns homocysteine into cysteine, the building block of glutathione. |
| Dehydroascorbate | Spent vitamin C, which glutathione recycles back into its working form. |
| LAT1 | The transporter that carries large amino acids into the brain; he says a phenylalanine and tyrosine backlog crowds it. |
| Glycation | Sugars bonding to proteins or amino acids without an enzyme; he ties it to deuterium and tissue "sludge." |
| Lattice janitor | His name for ubiquitin, which he says tags deuterium-loaded proteins for replacement. |
| Semiconductive transplant | His term for swapping a deuterium-loaded protein for a fresh one, washed by a burst of light protons from stored sugar. |
| Flash-wash | His term for sugar's quick burst of light protons used to clear deuterium in an emergency; fat is the long-game fuel. |
| PSA (prostate-specific antigen) | A protein-cutting enzyme also made by women's Skene's glands; he suggests it clears protein sludge in the pelvis. |
| Lumbo-sacral de-frag | His term for female ejaculation as a flush of deuterium from the lower spine and pelvic nerves. |
| Psilocin | The active form of psilocybin; he proposes it works partly by deuterium depletion. |
| Lithium-6 / lithium-7 | The two natural lithium isotopes; he says lithium-6 holds quantum coherence longer and acts as a dopant for the vagus. |
| Phased-array radar | His image for hair and skin as an antenna system tuned to the planet's field. |
| Magnetic castration | His phrase for shaving a man's beard, which he says removes an antenna anchor. |
| Electroculture | Using buried wires, magnets, antennas and stone to shape the soil's electrical and magnetic environment to boost plant growth. |
| Magnetic susceptibility | How strongly a material responds to a magnet; he says C4 plant ash raises the soil's. |
| Ice-nucleating proteins | Proteins that seed ice, and so rain, at relatively warm temperatures; he cites fungi that release them. |
| Bioprecipitation | Rain seeded by living things, such as fungal ice-nucleating proteins, which he calls the biosphere's backup rainmaker. |
| Melanized fungi | Fungi armored with melanin that he says survived past weak-field eras and keep the water cycle going. |
| Universal Stator | His term for a magnetic field that he says organizes living systems from the cosmos down to the cell. |
| Nephrite / jadeite | The two minerals sold as jade; translucent silicates he says act as light-shifting, piezoelectric "dopants" in teeth. |
| UV lighthouse | His image for a jade-inlaid tooth whose steadied faint light kills microbes. |
| Auricular VNS | Vagus nerve stimulation through the ear; he calls Maya jade its first form. |
| TMS | Transcranial magnetic stimulation; he calls Maya jade inlays an ancient version. |
| Vestibular migraine | Migraine with dizziness; he suggests a jade test to show when it is "solid-state." |
