## Glossary: Conditions

| Term | Plain meaning (in his usage) |
|---|---|
| Mitochondria | The cell's power plants, which he treats as electrical and magnetic devices |
| Inner mitochondrial membrane | The folded inner wall of the mitochondrion, which holds the strong electric field that powers the cell |
| Cell voltage (−70 vs −15 mV) | The charge across a cell's membrane: about −70 millivolts when healthy, −15 to −30 when failing |
| Deuterium | A heavy form of hydrogen with an extra neutron, which he says jams the cell's energy machinery |
| Deuterium-depleted water (DDW) | Water with less deuterium than normal, measured in parts per million (ppm) |
| ATP synthase | The spinning motor in mitochondria that makes ATP, the cell's energy currency |
| Complex IV | The last step of the energy chain, where oxygen is turned into water; he says it should run on copper-65 |
| Copper-65 | The heavier natural form of copper, which he says belongs in Complex IV and bone |
| UCP-2 | A gate in the inner membrane that he says leaks deuterium when cell voltage falls |
| Warburg shift | A cell falling back on fermentation (burning sugar without oxygen), which he treats as cancer's retreat |
| Matrix fire | His name for the chaotic damage inside a stalled mitochondrion: reactive oxygen and scrambled light |
| Safe mode | A lower-energy state (such as cancer or weight gain) that he says buys the body time |
| Triplet / singlet oxygen | Oxygen's stable, safe form / its reactive, damaging form |
| Ultra-weak photon emission | The faint light cells naturally give off, which he treats as a signal |
| nnEMF | Non-native electromagnetic fields from wireless devices, wiring and electronics |
| Grounding | Bare skin touching the earth (soil, sand, surf, grass) so the body shares its charge |
| Pinned / pinning | Being grounded, sunlit and living under a stable local magnetic field, which he says keeps oxygen and electrons properly aligned |
| Unpinned | Lacking that stable grounded, sunlit field setting |
| Spin pinning | The field keeping electrons' spins aligned so cell chemistry stays orderly |
| Magnetic excursion | A large, rapid weakening and shift of Earth's magnetic field |
| Glioma / glioblastoma | Brain tumors; glioblastoma is the most aggressive kind |
| Magnetic chemotherapy | Nighttime melatonin made in mitochondria, which he says pulls cancer cells back toward normal |
| Insulin resistance | Cells resisting insulin; to him a protective shield, not a defect |
| Glycerol-3-phosphate shuttle | A back-door route carrying light hydrogen into mitochondria, which he says runs during insulin resistance and leaves deuterium behind |
| Biophysical degausser | His name for high-dose insulin, which he says strips cell membranes of their charge |
| Beta cell | The pancreas cell that makes and releases insulin |
| Carbonic anhydrase | An enzyme that makes bicarbonate, which he says carries deuterium out of the pancreas |
| Vagal (bicarbonate) tailpipe | The vagus-driven flow of pancreatic bicarbonate that he says flushes deuterium out through the gut |
| Welded-shut tailpipe / exhaust | That flush blocked, so deuterium backs up into tissues |
| Marcus inversion / Marcus-inverted | The energy chain pushed past its optimum so electron flow stalls |
| Marcus inversion well | A place whose field holds a person in that stalled state |
| Dawn phenomenon | The early-morning rise in blood sugar, which he reads as a missed morning magnetic shift |
| Declination | The angle between compass north and true north at a place |
| Agonic line | A line where compass north equals true north (zero declination), which he treats as the most stable field |
| "Mammals who turn blue light into fat" | His phrase for storing unusable blue-light energy as deuterium in tissue and fat |
| Plasmapheresis | Filtering antibodies out of blood plasma |
| Bristol type 4 stool | A smooth, well-formed stool, which he uses as the sign the bicarbonate flush is working |
| Vagus nerve | The long nerve linking brain, heart and gut, which he treats as the body's deuterium exhaust cable |
| Cholesterol as insulator | His view that cholesterol stiffens and insulates membranes so they can hold charge |
| Summer antenna (sulfated cholesterol) | Sulfur-rich cholesterol carried in HDL that he says catches UV-B to make vitamin D3 |
| Winter/indoor form (LDL) | Sulfur-poor cholesterol that he says can't catch UV-B; high LDL signals light stress |
| Second magnetic fat | His name for cholesterol, which he says works with DHA as a spin filter |
| DHA | The omega-3 fat in seafood, which he calls a "magnetic fat" |
| CoQ10 | An electron carrier in the energy chain that statins also block |
| Quantum de-magnetizers | His name for statins, which he says strip the membrane's charge-holding machinery |
| Lp(a) | A cholesterol particle he sees as an emergency patch for a failing vessel wall |
| Carrington Event | The great solar storm of 1859, which he uses to date the field's decline |
| Melanin | The dark pigment he treats as a light-to-electricity converter and shield in skin, organs and arteries |
| Calcium score | A scan measure of calcium in the heart's arteries |

| Non-visual light sensing | Light detection by skin and tissue sensors outside vision |

| Neuromelanin | Iron-bound pigment in the brain's substantia nigra, which he calls a magnetic battery |
