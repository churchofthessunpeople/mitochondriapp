The practical routines Dr. Jack Kruse recommends, paraphrased from his own forum posts of Apr 11 – Oct 10, 2026; where he revised a routine, his latest version is given, and each entry cites its posts.
The app presents these as his recommendations, not as tested or endorsed advice.

## Light

### Sunrise light on bare eyes and skin
- **id:** sunrise-horizon
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** Be outside as the sun rises so its first light — which he describes as UV-A near 380 nm plus near-infrared — reaches bare eyes and skin. He says this dawn signal sets the body clock for the day, triggers the morning cortisol rise and starts the pancreas's bicarbonate flush of the gut.
- **steps:**
  1. Get up before sunrise so you are outside when the sun clears the horizon (in El Salvador he rises at 5:00 for a 5:25 sunrise).
  2. Take off sunglasses, contact lenses and glasses, and don't watch from behind window glass.
  3. Face east toward the rising sun, without staring straight into it.
  4. Uncover as much skin as you reasonably can.
  5. Stand barefoot at the same time — surf, wet sand, dewy grass or basalt (see barefoot-earth). His variants: "mammalian sphinxing" (facing east on all fours, grounded) or lying with the spine on the ground.
  6. Stay 30–45 minutes, within the first 60–90 minutes after sunrise; on the beach he sometimes stays three hours from sunrise.
  7. Repeat every morning.
  8. Watch for a well-formed (Bristol type 4) stool, which he takes as the sign the morning bicarbonate flush is working.
  9. For brain fog he pairs this routine with dropping chemical fixes and switching routers off before sleep.
- **equipment:** none
- **why:** sunrise-why
- **sources:** [382093](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382093/), [382666](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382666/), [383983](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/383983/), [377098](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/377098/), [374488](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/374488/)

### Midday sun and UV-B, unfiltered
- **id:** sun-exposure
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** Get regular midday sun, UV-B included, on bare skin with no sunscreen or sunglasses, stopping before any redness. He says UV-B protects a grounded, sun-adapted body rather than harming it, and that blocking the sun thins the skin.
- **steps:**
  1. Go out near solar noon, when your shadow is shorter than you are.
  2. Expose torso and back; tan with bare skin, not through clothing.
  3. Leave off sunscreen, sunglasses, contacts and glasses outdoors.
  4. Stand barefoot on the ground while you do it; he says strong UV without grounding and infrared can backfire.
  5. Build your time up gradually; move into shade or put on light clothing at the first warmth.
  6. Prioritize UV-B if you are not yet "pinned" (his term for being locked to a stable local magnetic field), and around the winter solstice.
  7. For dry skin, rely on morning sun, cold exposure and grounding rather than lotion or extra drinking water.
  8. After a shower use at most tallow or coconut oil — never petroleum- or silicone-based lotions or moisturizers with hidden UV filters.
  9. Go out on cloudy days too; he says they are no reason to stay in.
  10. Northern lineages (such as his own H) living in the tropics: avoid long exposure between 11 a.m. and 3 p.m. unless you are moving or grounded.
- **equipment:** none
- **his cautions:** Never burn on purpose; he reads a sunburn as proof you weren't pinned enough for the sun you got. He says sunscreen and sunglasses lead to skin atrophy. Leave UV-C lamps alone.
- **why:** solar-noon-vitamin-d, melanin-semiconductor
- **sources:** [382093](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382093/), [383426](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/383426/), [378979](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/378979/), [377533](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/377533/), [376133](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/376133/)

### Four-phase sun day (building a "solar callus")
- **id:** four-phase-sun-day
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** His timed daily light schedule for a person of northern maternal lineage (his own, haplogroup H) adapting to tropical sun. He says taking each part of the solar day in order builds a "solar callus" — skin adapted to strong sun — without burning.
- **steps:**
  1. Sunrise phase: within 60–90 minutes after the sun clears the horizon, spend 30–45 minutes in its deep red and near-infrared light (no UV yet) with bare eyes and as much bare skin as possible, feet on volcanic basalt or wet black sand.
  2. Mid-morning UV-A phase (about 8:30–11:00, depending on season and place): 15–25 minutes with skin and eyes open to the sky while walking or moving; never sunglasses, contacts or glasses.
  3. Midday UV-B phase (shadow shorter than your height, about 11:30–1:30): 3–10 minutes at most on torso and back while grounded on bare rock.
  4. If you're not yet adapted, start the midday phase at 3 minutes and add 1 minute every few days as your tan deepens.
  5. At the first slight sting or warmth, move into shade or put on long, light clothing.
  6. Sunset phase: in the last hour of daylight, 20–30 minutes of skin exposure to red and infrared (no UV) while winding down.
  7. Even once adapted, avoid artificial light at night and out-of-season food eaten under the noon sun.
  8. Optional: saffron, which he says speeds the callus.
- **equipment:** none (basalt or black sand ideal)
- **his cautions:** Never let skin redden. He says a northern lineage plunged straight into tropical UV gets damaged, so acclimate gradually.
- **why:** solar-callus, haplogroup-zipcode
- **sources:** [382093](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382093/), [382091](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382091/), [382089](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382089/)

### Evening light: incandescent, red, dark
- **id:** blue-light-hygiene
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** After sunset keep light warm and free of blue; in daylight never block blue. He says blue light after dark disrupts the body clock and the melanin in eyes and skin, while daytime blue is part of the natural signal.
- **steps:**
  1. Replace LED and fluorescent bulbs with incandescent ones.
  2. In daylight, wear no blue blockers of any tint, red or yellow included; he says they act on the body clock like a drug.
  3. By day, open a window rather than buying lamps that imitate the sun.
  4. After sunset, switch to red incandescent bulbs or wear glasses that block 100% of blue and green light.
  5. Don't eat sugary food under screens or LEDs at night.
  6. Never wear sunglasses. If you wear contact lenses, go back to glasses, and take the glasses off whenever you're outdoors; he says vagus nerve branches surface around the eye muscles, so filtering light at the eye dulls vagal tone.
  7. Sleep in total darkness, with no artificial light at night.
- **equipment:** incandescent and red bulbs; 100% blue-blocking glasses
- **why:** blue-light-hygiene
- **sources:** [382553](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382553/), [382679](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382679/), [381470](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/381470/), [382234](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382234/), [372047](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/372047/)

## Water

### Drinking water: reverse osmosis plus UV
- **id:** drinking-water
- **pillar:** water
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** Drink reverse-osmosis water with a UV stage, or clean deep-source water, and avoid hard, mineral-heavy, fluoridated or municipal water. He says where water comes from and how it is piped matter, and that surface water is getting heavier in deuterium.
- **steps:**
  1. Filter drinking water by reverse osmosis followed by a UV stage.
  2. Avoid fluoride; he ranks it worse than iodine in an unstable field.
  3. Avoid municipal water lines in heavily wired urban areas, and surface-runoff river water.
  4. Don't rehydrate on hard, metal-laden well water.
  5. Prefer deep "primary water" (from fissures in volcanic rock) or ancient artesian wells to surface water; clean local spring water drunk in the sun is his natural source of minerals such as magnesium. Judge a bottled spring water (he discussed Mountain Valley) by the hydrology of its source; desert sources count against it.
  6. Never drink from the Riviera Maya cenote system; on Central America's Pacific side, make a new water plan, since he says local rain is getting heavier in deuterium.
  7. Prefer copper plumbing to plastic, which he says strips water's charge.
  8. Store drinking water in large copper vessels and let it steep 4–16 hours; he says the copper surface favors light hydrogen over deuterium.
  9. If local animals show signs of decline, switch to deep aquifer water (see track-your-field).
- **equipment:** RO filter with UV stage; optional copper storage vessels
- **why:** deuterium-and-water
- **sources:** [383948](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/383948/), [383693](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/383693/), [381072](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/381072/), [378274](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/378274/), [373835](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/373835/)

  7. To check a water's deuterium, send a sample to a water company or chemical lab.

- **his cautions:** Deuterium is secondary: it matters once magnetic or electrical interference has damaged the cell, so location and light come first. Feeling worse at first is expected in a deuterium-loaded body, he says, and isn't a reason to conclude DDW is wrong for you.
