## Glossary: Light and Water

| Term | Plain meaning (in his usage) |
|---|---|
| UV-A | The gentler ultraviolet just beyond violet; he treats dawn UV-A near 380 nm as the master clock signal |
| UV-B | The shorter, stronger ultraviolet of the midday sun that makes vitamin D in skin |
| Near-infrared (NIR) | Invisible light just past red, roughly 600–1000 nm, that reaches deep into tissue |
| nm (nanometre) | The unit used to name a colour (wavelength) of light |
| Mitochondria | The cell's power plants, which he treats as light- and field-driven devices |
| Inner mitochondrial membrane | The folded membrane inside mitochondria where the energy chain sits and voltage is held |
| Energy chain (Complexes I–IV) | The four-step chain in mitochondria that passes electrons along and ends by turning oxygen into water |
| Complex I | The entry point of the energy chain; he calls it a UV-A-driven node |
| Complex II | A second entry point that takes electrons from fat burning and needs very precise spacing |
| Complex III / Q-cycle | The chain's middle step; he calls its recycling loop a "deli slicer" that shaves light frequencies off food electrons |
| Complex IV | The last step, where oxygen becomes water; his "isotope mass gate" that makes deuterium-depleted water |
| ATP synthase | The rotary motor in mitochondria (about 9,000 RPM in his figure) that makes ATP |
| ATP | The cell's energy currency |
| Optico-magnetic | His name for light's non-visual route through eyes and skin to the brain's hormone centres |
| Neuropsin (OPN5) | A UV-A sensor in eye and skin he treats as the master clock input |
| Melanopsin | A blue-sensing light receptor in the eye that informs the body clock |
| Dual-axis transistor | His description of neuropsin reading both sunlight and Earth's magnetic field |
| Zeeman handshake | Sunlight and the magnetic field agreeing so oxygen and electron spins stay in order |
| Triplet oxygen | Oxygen's normal, stable, safe form |
| Singlet oxygen | Oxygen's energised, reactive form that damages membranes |
| Molecular buzzsaw | His image for singlet oxygen burning through membranes |
| Intersystem crossing | A forced spin flip in the energy chain that turns safe oxygen into singlet oxygen |
| NAD⁺ | A molecule that carries electrons and fuels repair enzymes; he treats it as light-storing |
| NADD+ | His term for NAD⁺ loaded with deuterium |
| SIRT1 | A repair enzyme that runs on NAD⁺ |
| mTOR / "mTOR switch" | The cell's build-or-clean-up regulator; he calls 380 nm light the switch that sets it and calls mTOR the cell's deuterium sensor |
| Heme | The iron-holding core of hemoglobin and of the energy chain's cytochromes |
| POMC | A hormone precursor the body cuts into alpha-MSH, the signal that makes melanin |
| Alpha-MSH | The hormone signal that tells skin and hair cells to make melanin |
| MITF | The control switch of pigment cells; he reads greying hair as its failure |
| Melatonin | The night hormone; he says mitochondria also make it by day using UV |
| Cortisol awakening response | The natural rise in cortisol after waking |
| Bicarbonate flush | The roughly two litres of bicarbonate the pancreas releases daily, which he says sweeps deuterium out through the gut |
| Glucagon gene network | Genes he says sunrise switches on to start the bicarbonate flush |
| FOXO3 | A longevity gene he says morning sun lets brake cell division to protect the genome |
| Bristol type 4 | A well-formed stool; his sign the morning flush is working |
| Pinned / unpinned | Living grounded and sunlit under a stable, flat local magnetic field, or not |
| Grounding | Bare skin contact with the earth, sea or wet rock |
| nnEMF | Non-native electromagnetic fields: wireless, wiring, electronics |
| Marcus inversion | Electron flow stalling once it is pushed past its optimum |
| Marcus crash | His name for a keto flu that never lifts because fat-derived electrons pile up in a stalled chain |
| Term life insurance | His phrase for UV-B protecting unpinned people by braking the energy chain |
| Dual brake | UV-B (via the vitamin D receptor) and UV-A (via nitric oxide) slowing the chain to keep it out of Marcus inversion |
| Radical pair | Two molecular fragments, each with one unpaired electron, whose spins decide the reaction's outcome |
| Lumisterol / tachysterol | The by-products sun-made vitamin D turns into once enough is made |
| Vitamin D "thermostat" | The self-limiting cutoff in skin that he says makes sun-made vitamin D impossible to overdose |
| Sulfated HDL / LDL format | Summer cholesterol tuned to UV-B versus winter, electron-poor cholesterol that can't use it |
| Mismatch Freeze | Skin cholesterol stuck in its winter (LDL) format after years of blue light and nnEMF |
| Atomic impurity | A foreign compound (here, pill vitamin D) acting as a disruptive "dopant" in cells |
| Solar callus | Skin and tissue adapted to strong sun so they tan rather than burn |
| Haplogroup | A maternal lineage traced through mitochondrial DNA |
| H lineage ("leaky") | His northern European haplogroup, whose mitochondria he says shed heat rather than couple tightly |
| Re-coupled relocation | Moving to the tropics slowly while grounding locally so mitochondria adapt |
| Four-phase sun day | His daily sequence: sunrise red/infrared, mid-morning UV-A, brief midday UV-B, sunset red/infrared |
| Saffron compounds | Crocin, crocetin, safranal and kaempferol, which he says speed the solar callus |
| Structured water / exclusion zone (EZ) | Water ordered into a charge-holding gel layer beside surfaces |
| 160 vs 78 | His numbers for structured, charge-holding cell water (160) versus ordinary water (78) |
| Command channel | His description of red light as the sun's control signal for light hydrogen |
| 43% red light engine | Red and infrared (about 43% of sunlight) spinning the liver's ATP synthase on light hydrogen |
| 0.66 eV | The energy he says matches near-infrared photons and the chain's tunnelling barrier |
| Photonic purge | Morning near-infrared knocking nitric oxide off Complex IV so oxygen can enter |
| NIRDS | Near-Infrared Deficiency Syndrome: too little red/infrared light, or being unable to use it |
| Lubricates rather than drives | His summary that infrared eases the motor but can't align electron spins |
| Paramagnetic lock | Membranes, cholesterol rafts and structured water ordered enough to use light and keep oxygen stable |
| Methylene blue trap | His warning about pairing methylene blue with red light in an ungrounded, wireless-heavy setting |
| Biological quantum semiconductor | His description of melanin as a material that turns light into current |
| Fenton reaction | Iron-driven chemistry that makes destructive radicals |
| Capacitor | Something that stores and releases electrical charge |
| Neuromelanin / interior tan | Brain melanin, which he says matters more than skin tan |
| Rusted melanin / lipofuscin | Melanin degraded into debris that clogs sleep centres |
| Quinolinic acid shunt | Broken-down melanin feeding a pathway that silences BDNF |
| BDNF | The brain's growth and repair signal |
| Eccrine glands | Sweat glands; he calls them water sorters that should build a structured film on skin |
| Oxidation field | The layer of hydroxyl radicals the skin makes at its surface, which lotions smother |
| Lanthanides | Rare-earth metals he says melanin binds and uses to turn radiation into current |
| Carry its own sun | Melanised life making its own current through long darkness |
| K–T impact | The asteroid strike that ended the dinosaurs |
| PER2 / lipid rafts | A core clock protein and the fatty membrane patches that anchor it |
| Reality subtraction | Screens and algorithms replacing perception of the real world |
| "Like a drug" (daytime blue blockers) | Changing the body's own feedback loops the way a medication does |
| Deuterium | Heavy hydrogen: hydrogen with an extra neutron, twice as heavy |
| Deuteron | The nucleus of a deuterium atom |
| Light hydrogen (protium) | Ordinary hydrogen without the extra neutron |
| DDW | Deuterium-depleted water |
| ppm | Parts per million; tap water is about 150 ppm deuterium in his figures |
| Isotope mass gate | Complex IV making water that excludes deuterium |
| Horizontal-field sorting | A strong, stable horizontal field separating light hydrogen from deuterium |
| Primary water | Deep "new" water (Stephan Riess's idea) that never evaporated and is low in deuterium |
| Reverse osmosis + UV | His preferred water filtration: a fine membrane filter followed by UV treatment |
| Plastic static trap | Plastic pipes stripping water's charge |
| Lenard effect | Charge separation when water splashes or breaks, which he says restructures it |
| CISS | Spin filtering of electrons by one-handed (chiral) molecules |
| Chiral | One-handed, like a left or right glove |
| Spin-phase wrecking ball | Deuterium's nuclear spin drifting out of step with spin-filtering proteins |
| Zero-point energy | The minimum vibration energy of a chemical bond |
| Tunnelling | A particle slipping through an energy barrier by a quantum effect |
| Tunnelling "tax" | The extra energy cost deuterium pays to move compared with light hydrogen |
| Kinetic isotope effect | Heavier isotopes reacting more slowly |
| Strips the gears | Deuterium deforming ATP synthase's rotor so it stutters |

| Vortex centrifuge | Spinning blood and spinal fluid flinging deuterium-heavy water outward |
| Autophagy / apoptosis | The cell's self-recycling / orderly cell death |
