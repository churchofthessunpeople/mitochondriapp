## Light

### Sunrise light on bare eyes and skin
- **id:** sunrise-horizon
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** Be outside as the sun rises so dawn light (he calls it UV-A near 380 nm plus near-infrared) reaches bare eyes and skin.
- **steps:**
  1. Be out as the sun clears the horizon; he uses the first 60–90 minutes and stays 30–45 minutes.
  2. Nothing between you and the sun: no sunglasses, contacts, glasses or window glass.
  3. Uncover as much skin as you reasonably can.
  4. Stand barefoot at the same time — surf, wet sand, dewy grass or basalt.
  5. He gives this routine, plus routers off at night, for brain fog.
  6. He says the first horizon light starts the pancreas's morning bicarbonate flush; a well-formed (Bristol type 4) stool is his sign it's working.
- **equipment:** none

### Midday sun and UV-B, unfiltered
- **id:** sun-exposure
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** Get regular midday sun, UV-B included, on bare skin with no sunscreen or sunglasses, stopping before any redness.
- **steps:**
  1. Near solar noon (shadow shorter than you), expose torso and back; tan bare-skinned, not through clothes.
  2. No sunscreen, sunglasses, contacts or glasses outdoors.
  3. Build up gradually; seek shade or cover up at the first warmth.
  4. Prioritize UV-B if you are not "pinned" (locked to a stable local magnetic field — his term), and around the winter solstice.
  5. Ground while you do it; he says strong UV without grounding and infrared can backfire.
  6. Dry skin: morning sun, cold and grounding, not lotion; after a shower at most tallow or coconut oil, never petroleum, silicone or UV-filter lotions.
- **equipment:** none
- **his cautions:** Never burn on purpose; he reads a sunburn as proof you weren't pinned enough.
- **why:** solar-noon-vitamin-d, melanin-semiconductor
- **sources:** [382093](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/382093/), [383426](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/383426/), [378979](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/378979/), [377533](https://forum.jackkruse.com/posts/377533/)

### Four-phase sun day (building a "solar callus")
- **id:** four-phase-sun-day
- **pillar:** light
- **app:** ready
- **summary:** His timed daily light schedule for a northern-lineage person (his own maternal line, H) adapting to tropical sun.
- **steps:**
  1. Sunrise (first 60–90 minutes): 30–45 minutes of red and near-infrared on bare eyes and maximum skin, feet on basalt or wet black sand.

  5. No artificial night light or out-of-season food alongside it.
  6. Optional: saffron, which he says speeds the callus (skin adapted to strong sun).
- **equipment:** none (basalt or black sand ideal)
- **his cautions:** Never push to redness; he says a northern lineage plunged into tropical UV gets damaged.
